# Chapter 1. Introduction – AI Tutors

## What is an AI Tutor?
AI Tutors are applications that simulate human-like teaching. They provide personalized educational experiences with appropriate instructional support, tailored lessons, and student feedback. AI Tutors use intelligent tutoring systems that are sophisticated applications of AI in personalized learning. They can adjust the content individually for students' unique needs, such as their learning pace, disabilities like visual impairments, and language barriers.

AI Tutors can have various functions. The main standard functions that enable a personalized learning experience are:

- Understanding and responding to students’ inputs and simulating human language
- The adjustment of difficulty and style of instructions according to the personal pace of the students
- Performing voice-based interactions with speech recognition to talk with students

### Examples of AI Tutors:
- ChatGPT, for example, is often used as a tutor but is not explicitly designed for learning purposes.
- Applications like Duolingo MAX integrate GPT-4, the technology used for ChatGPT.

# Chapter 2. Specifics of AI Tutors

### 1. Which technology is used to meet the student's unique needs?
AI Tutors integrate multiple AI technologies to perform their tasks. This section will explain technologies that help AI tutors simulate a human-like conversation and provide personalized learning material. Natural Language Processing (NLP) helps generate language that makes dialogues human-like. AI tutors combine this technology with Machine Learning and Deep Learning models.

Adaptive Learning Technology uses Machine Learning, Deep Learning algorithms, and data analytics to customize the learning experience.

### 2. Adaptive Learning Technologies
The key to ALT is the dynamic adjustment of content delivery, assessment, and feedback. The AI system can react to the student's learning capacity in real-time. The technologies that are needed are Machine Learning and Deep Learning. Chatbots also play a vital role.

### 3. How does it work?
Adaptive learning software assesses a student’s mastery of a concept or skill in real-time, then dynamically adjusts the next lesson or practice. AI tutors collect data from student interactions, such as responses, time spent on tasks, and the accuracy of answers. AI tutors analyze them with ML algorithms in real-time. The AI Tutor can then create a student profile based on their knowledge level, learning style, and areas of difficulty. It is possible to personalize the content, adjust pacing and progression, give feedback, and assess the students.

# Chapter 3. Implications

### 1. How AI Tutors help reduce disadvantages
It is possible to provide high-quality education personalized to the students’ needs. Significantly, the needs of students with learning difficulties or impairments can be well met. Also, disadvantaged students can be provided with a system that helps them close their specific learning gaps. Teachers have more time to focus on personalized instructions because AI Tutors take over tasks like giving feedback. Besides these evident and important chances AI Tutors offer, some ethical questions arise.

### 2. Irreplaceable human qualities
Human tutors can have genuine empathy and understanding for their students. They see a student's background and unique needs, context, and personal situation and can pedagogically react to the student’s circumstances. Teachers can enhance students’ social skills and see social qualities not measurable for AI tutors.

Applications must be easily accessible to everyone. If they are only accessible to specific regions and very expensive, inequalities are reinforced. Disadvantages also affect groups in society that don’t have access to the technical infrastructure due to age or socioeconomic status.

### 4. Bias & Privacy Issues
Using Machine Learning and Deep Learning poses the same problems discussed in Webinar 1. Depending on the data set used to train an AI Tutor, biases can be exacerbated, and students might not be provided with the most accurate answers. For example, an AI Tutor can provide examples or scenarios showing a context or perspective unrelatable to some students’ lives. This can hinder their learning and decrease their confidence in learning. Privacy issues play an important role too: Students have to share much personal information, such as grades, learning habits, and behavior patterns. This raises the risk of privacy breaches, affecting trust in schools and educational platforms.

### 5. AI compass for good and fair use of AI Tutors in the educational context
What impact can the use of an AI tutor have? To navigate the intricacies of this concern, we offer a set of questions for reflection and discussion amongst teachers, school heads, and other education professionals. We invite you to answer these questions and write your own compass for a good and fair use of AI Tutors in your institution.

- Could the widespread use of AI tutors exacerbate existing inequalities in education? If so, how?
- What are the potential ethical concerns if AI tutors are only accessible to those with certain resources?
- What are the potential consequences of reducing face-to-face interaction between students and teachers?
- What are the risks of AI tutors perpetuating or amplifying biases present in the data they are trained on?
- How do we assess the fairness of AI tutors when they may perpetuate systemic inequalities?
- What level of transparency is necessary for AI tutors to maintain user trust?
- How can students' privacy be safeguarded in an environment where AI tutors continuously collect data?
- Who is responsible for an AI tutor’s incorrect information or advice that negatively impacts students’ learning?
- How does AI Teaching affect the relationship between human educators and students? What might be the positive and negative consequences?
- What does it mean for students to have all their learning data collected? Can they be easily controlled by the teachers? Do they win or lose autonomy?
- What can you do to protect your data when using AI applications?
