Some undifferentiated and sensationalist media coverage of GenAI creates a distorted image of what
GenAI is, where it is used, and its capabilities. The media hype about the potential of GenAI blurs
the public understanding of where the technology is used and how it works. This is also due to the
rapid development of new GenAI tools across a wide spectrum of fields we engage with on an everyday
basis. However, in most fields such as medicine, the use of GenAI is still at an experimental stage
and part of basic research with the long-term aim to create reliable and robust diagnosis tools.
