The film “AI and your job” uses the example of a job interview with a computer to show how AI is increasingly influencing working life and draws attention to the potential and challenges of the technology behind it.

### Algorithms in Hiring

Hiring is challenging across all industries, with employers often receiving hundreds of applications for a single position, especially in corporate roles. To handle this, many companies now use HR technology with algorithms throughout the hiring process to make it faster, smarter, and more efficient.

### ADM and e-recruiting

The reason companies use AI-powered hiring tools is to minimize time-to-hire averages and consequently reduce their HR expenses.
Recruiting platforms start by using algorithms to decide who will see specific job postings. Later, recruiters may use questionnaires, tests, resume scanning, or even AI-based games to pick who moves forward or receives an offer.

Another type of algorithm works behind the scenes to quickly sort applicant information, helping recruiters get candidates into interviews as fast as possible.
