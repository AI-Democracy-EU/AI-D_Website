Before starting with the [impact section of this webinar](./impact), we recommend taking some time and doing small exercises.

- We all must go through an application process at some point. That's why we chose the scenario for the exercises.
- The simulation of application processes raises central questions of the modern working world that almost everyone experiences sooner or later.
- The aim of the first exercise ([Resource1](../../w3/Resource1.pdf)) is to first evaluate applications ourselves. In the second exercise ([Resource2](../../w3/Resource2.pdf)) we use a simulation to understand how an AI can proceed to help recruiters in the process.
- In the end of resource 2, we would like to encourage reflection on the criteria and possible biases to critically scrutinize issues such as anti-discrimination and fairness in the world of work.

Go to the [resources](../algorithms-in-e-recruiting-software#resources) and start them right away. Otherwise, you can also continue directly with the [impact section](./impact).
