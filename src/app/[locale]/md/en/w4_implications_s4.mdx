- Using Machine Learning and Deep Learning poses the same problems discussed in Webinar 1. Depending on the data set used to train an AI Tutor, biases can be exacerbated, and students might not be provided with the most accurate answers. For example, an AI Tutor can provide examples or scenarios showing a context or perspective unrelatable to some students’ lives. This can hinder their learning and decrease their confidence in learning.
- Privacy issues play a critical role, too: Students have to share personal information such as grades, learning habits, and behaviour patterns. This raises the risk of privacy breaches, affecting trust in schools and educational platforms.
