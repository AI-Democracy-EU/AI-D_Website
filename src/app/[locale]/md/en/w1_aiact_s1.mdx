AI can automate tasks, optimise processes, and provide decision-making support, improving efficiency across various fields.

- AI can take over a lot of tasks with **automation**. It is possible to work more efficiently with the help of AI Systems. AI can also optimise processes, such as visualisation and analysis. That means AI Systems can simplify labour and automate repetitive tasks.
- AI can provide **decision-making** support for complex tasks.
- AI can help **reduce disadvantages**, e.g. as a tutor. Students have unique learning needs, and AI Systems can provide a learning experience designed to meet them.
