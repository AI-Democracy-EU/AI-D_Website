- AI Tutors are applications that support teaching by providing personalised educational experiences with appropriate instruction support, tailored lessons and student feedback.
- AI Tutors use intelligent tutoring systems that are sophisticated applications of AI in personalised learning.
- They can adjust the content individually for students' unique needs, such as their learning pace, disabilities like visual impairments, and language barriers…
- AI Tutors can have various functions. Mainly standard functions that enable a personalised learning experience are:
  - Simple interaction in natural language (like chatbots)
  - he adjustment of difficulty and style of instructions according to the personal pace of the students
  - Perform voice-based interactions with speech recognition to talk with students
