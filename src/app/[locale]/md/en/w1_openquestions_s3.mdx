- Large generative AI models use a lot of energy. Data centres are the facilities that house the computer servers that process the data on which AI applications run. [Studies](https://ris.utwente.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/252516283/1_s2.0_S0306261921003019_main.pdf) estimate that the energy consumption of data centres on the European continent will [grow by 28 % by 2030](https://news.climate.columbia.edu/2023/06/09/ais-growing-carbon-footprint/).
- Despite their vast carbon footprint, AI systems can also help fight climate change: they can help develop new materials, e.g. those for wind turbines, or they can optimise the use of renewable energy with complex weather calculations.
