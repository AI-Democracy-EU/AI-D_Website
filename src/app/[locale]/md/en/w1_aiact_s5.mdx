How do politics and democracy react to AI challenges? How do they ensure that AI is used best?
- You just learned that, even though AI has a lot of potential and positive aspects, risks must be managed.
- Political decision-makers try to come up with rules for governments and private companies to ensure that AI is used responsibly.

Political regulation of AI ensures:
- Democratic control
- Transparency, Accountability, Inclusiveness, and respect for Human Rights
- No misuse of users’ private and sensitive data
