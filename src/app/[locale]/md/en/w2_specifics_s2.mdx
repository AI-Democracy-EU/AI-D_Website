The main feature of GenAI is to recombine already existing data and present it in a new form because of a given task formulated as a prompt. LLMs are commonly used for tasks like content creation, editing, or sorting. Although often suggested, LLMs are not a good tool for research, analysis, or prediction so far. Their use for these tasks is controversially discussed as the results often consist of false information and do not rely on valid sources.

Most applications use base models, such as ChatGPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google), Claude (Anthropic), Llama (Meta), and Mistral. These models are often retrained with specific data or combined with other models for tasks like text and image generation. They function by predicting the next word or token, with some models able to handle up to 128,000 tokens (as of 2024 and according to OpenAI) between input and output. Tokens are pieces of words, and before processing, inputs are broken down into tokens. These tokens aren’t always cut at exact word boundaries - they may include spaces or parts of words. A helpful way to understand token length is that 1 token is approximately equal to 4 characters in English or about ¾ of a word.
