These are some pressing questions for democracy. We invite you to reflect on them.
- **Automated Decision Making**
To what extent should AI be allowed to make decisions without human intervention, especially in critical areas like healthcare or criminal justice?
How can we ensure that AI-driven ADM processes are transparent so that individuals affected by these decisions can understand the criteria and logic used?
- **Bias**
How can we ensure that AI systems are trained on data free from historical and systemic biases? Is it even possible to create unbiased data?
What does it mean for an AI system to be "fair"?
- **Social disparities**
How do disparities in access to AI technologies contribute to widening the gap between different social groups? How might AI systems reinforce existing social disparities, such as those related to race, gender, or socioeconomic status?
How can AI technologies be designed and implemented to avoid deepening the digital divide, ensuring that all communities benefit equally from AI advancements?
