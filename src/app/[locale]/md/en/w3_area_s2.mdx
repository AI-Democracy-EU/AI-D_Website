### Example 1: Video Interview Analysis

One big AI Hire system called HireVue analyzes for example candidates' facial expressions, word choice, and speaking voice during video interviews to assess their suitability for roles.

### Example 2: Resume, Initial Screening and onboarding by ChatBots

The company Unilever uses a chatbot named 'Unabot' to conduct initial screenings of candidates, asking them questions about their qualifications and experience before scheduling human interviews.
