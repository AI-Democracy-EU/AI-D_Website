Even though our democratic system works well, some factors make the democratic decision-making process around AI especially difficult. The development of AI technologies is currently in the hands of big tech companies and lacks democratic control.

**Power of large companies:** Since large companies develop AI Systems, they decide what technologies are developed and who can access their applications. Often, this is a question of material resources.

**Transparency:** Because the AI companies are private, there is a lack of data, e.g. on the energy use of large AI applications. This makes it difficult to react with the appropriate political measures.

**Rapid technological development:** Regulations like the EU AI Act are necessary. However, there is a time lag between the democratic decision-making process that leads to the regulations and the pace of the technology process. That makes it challenging to control new developments democratically.
