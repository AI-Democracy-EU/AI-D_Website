Government authorities and private firms like insurance companies and banks use AI technologies such as automated decision-making, social scoring and surveillance.
The use of AI technologies has to be shaped by democratic mechanisms. That means that their use has to be closely monitored and accompanied by legislation that minimises its risks.

In democratic societies, we must discuss reliable AI technology, the power behind the use of AI technology, checks and balances, justice, fairness, non-discrimination, education and protection of human rights.
