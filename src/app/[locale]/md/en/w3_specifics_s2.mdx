Unconscious biases and discrimination prevalent in society can also appear in AI systems. If there is a bias in the data, the AI will adapt the given bias. This happens because people make decisions in developing and applying AI, including data selection, cleansing, and weighting.

At this point, it is important to attach importance to the objectivity of criteria to minimize human prejudices.
