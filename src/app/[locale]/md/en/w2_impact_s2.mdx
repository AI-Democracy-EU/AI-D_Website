The political landscape is increasingly vulnerable to the disruptive potential of GenAI. As deepfakes become more sophisticated, it poses a significant threat to the integrity of democratic processes. Political campaigns and elections are particularly susceptible to AI-generated disinformation, with deepfake videos and audio clips of candidates making false statements or engaging in fabricated scandals becoming more prevalent. This technology can be weaponised to sway public opinion and discredit political opponents. The ease with which GenAI can produce convincing fake news articles at scale further exacerbates the spread of misinformation, making it challenging for voters to discern fact from fiction. As a result, trust in political institutions and media sources can erode, potentially leading to increased voter apathy and societal polarisation. The rapid dissemination of AI-generated content on social media platforms amplifies these issues, creating echo chambers that reinforce existing biases and hinder constructive political discourse.
