We have seen in the first two chapters of this webinar that one main problem of ADM and AI systems is algorithmic bias. This can also lead to errors or unfair results in e-recruiting systems. They can among others reinforce existing social inequalities if they are trained on unbalanced data.

At the same time, generative AI tools can help with the creation of CVs and cover letters and provide feedback on interview preparation. At the same time, it is also questionable whether this is really an advantage or whether it leads to mainstreaming as every application and every CV looks similar then.

In the last section of the webinar, we want to stress the impact, reflect with you on regulatory and ethical considerations and give you practical guidance through an AI compass.
