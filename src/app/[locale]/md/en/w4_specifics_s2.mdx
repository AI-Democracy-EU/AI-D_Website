- Adaptive learning software assesses a student's mastery of a concept or skill in real-time.
- Then, it dynamically adjusts the next lesson or practice.
- AI tutors collect data from student interactions, such as responses, time spent on tasks, and the accuracy of answers.
- AI tutors analyse the data with ML algorithms in real-time.
- The AI Tutor can then create a student profile based on their knowledge level, learning style, and areas of difficulty.
- Personalising the content, adjusting pacing and progression, giving feedback and assessing the students is possible.
