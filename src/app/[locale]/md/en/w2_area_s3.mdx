The different GenAI models we all encounter through chatbots such as ChatGPT or music creation such as Suno all have in common that they can be used through interaction based on Natural Language. GenAI in general generates outputs in various media formats following an input such as a prompt given to a chatbot. The output in Large Language Models (LLM) varies from text-to-text (translation, summarization, Q&As, writing assistants), over text-to-image (picture and video generation), and text-to-audio (music, sounds, speech) to text-to-decision (computer games).
