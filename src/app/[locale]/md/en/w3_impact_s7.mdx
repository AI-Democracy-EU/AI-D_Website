We make it transparent within the team, including full-time and voluntary staff, who use AI and for what purpose. Nobody is pressured into using it.

We use AI to make our work easier. We take a self-critical approach to the increased performance expectations often associated with this.

We actively observe and reflect on whether and how AI changes the way we work together as a team. Social relationships remain more important than efficiency.

We do not use AI to evaluate performance.
