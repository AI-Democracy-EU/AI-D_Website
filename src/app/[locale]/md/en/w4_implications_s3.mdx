- AI Applications must be easily accessible to
  everyone. If they are only accessible to specific regions and very expensive, inequalities are
  reinforced.
- Disadvantages also affect groups in society that don’t have access to the technical
  infrastructure due to age or socioeconomic status.
- Some scholars use the term digital divide to
  describe these inequalities. Since AI has gained importance over the years, the term AI divide
  describes “unequal access, benefits, and opportunities in AI technology across various regions,
  communities, and socioeconomic groups.” ([Gonzales, 2024](../bibliography#w4-3))
