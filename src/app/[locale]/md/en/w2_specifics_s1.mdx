As established, GenAI refers to systems that create content like text, images, music, or code based on trained models and user prompts. These systems rely on Machine Learning (ML) of patterns through enormous amounts of data, allowing them to generate new patterns by “guessing” based on statistical models and probabilities. This can include text, language, music, images, code, or any other form that can be mapped into patterns. Bots were trained with countless data from various sources, particularly from the net and primarily from the global North and in English.

### How do LLMs work?

However, LLMs often operate without specific guidelines on how something should be understood or interpreted. To address this, they incorporate human feedback and ethical rules to refine their outputs and ensure they align with user expectations. AI prompting is the input of specific instructions to guide the AI's output, such as generating text or images, enabling users to influence and adjust the results.
