- Human tutors can have genuine empathy and understanding for their students.
- They see a student's background and unique needs, context, and personal situation and can pedagogically react to the student’s circumstances.
- Teachers can enhance students’ social skills and see social qualities not measurable for AI tutors.
- Learning with other students is an integral part of the learning experience. It prevents exclusion and a loss of social interaction.
- Using AI Tutors can be an appropriate measure to enrich learning, but they cannot replace the qualities of a human teacher.
