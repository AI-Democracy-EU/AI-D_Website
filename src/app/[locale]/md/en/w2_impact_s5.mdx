[In the first webinar](../fundamentals-of-ai) of this series, you are informed about the EU AI Act. The overarching aim is “to improve the functioning of the internal market and promote the uptake of human-centric and trustworthy artificial intelligence (AI), while ensuring a high level of protection of health, safety, fundamental rights enshrined in the Charter, including democracy, the rule of law and environmental protection, against the harmful effects of AI systems in the Union and supporting innovation” ([Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 1: Subject Matter](../bibliography#w2-12)). The Act is primarily targeted at the industry developing AI models. Ideally, the AI Act will lead to answers to all of the questions raised on the last slide and increase the positive aspects of GenAI’s impact. However, as learned in the first webinar, it is still open how these regulations will be implemented. It is therefore inevitable to teach and learn critical AI competencies in all disciplines which includes training in good and responsible use of the technology.
