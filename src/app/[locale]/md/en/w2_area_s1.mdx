### Potentials of GenAI

As the video “GenAI” explains, generative AI (GenAI) can be a powerful tool for creating text, images, and sounds that are very similar to those created by humans. Additionally, GenAI can relieve humans of monotonous tasks through automation, although it still requires human supervision to achieve meaningful results.

### Limitations and Risks of GenAI

However, the video already highlights some limitations, the potential for misuse in spreading disinformation, ethical concerns in the creative industries, and the importance of human oversight and regulation. This is due to the sometimes unexpected or erroneous results, as AI has no reasoning ability of its own and merely reconstructs statistical patterns and is unable to capture nuance and depth. An unreflective and unregulated use of GenAI can consequently lead to disinformation and political manipulation, such as deepfakes.
During this webinar, you will be familiarized with the workings, potential, and limitations as well as risks of GenAI in more detail and its societal impact.
