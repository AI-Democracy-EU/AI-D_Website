In the picture above, you see some examples of applications that use AI-Systems and involve a lot of different machine learning techniques:

- Chatbots and generative AI.
- Language translation, robotics.
- Image and voice generation.
- Content production in the media (like semi-automated text production)
- in the medicine and health sector (e.g. automated image analysis to detect cancer).
- In manufacturing, logistics, self-driving cars, etc.
