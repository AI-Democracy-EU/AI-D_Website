- The training of AI is not fully automated but involves a lot of manual work by humans. Human labour is needed to build AI-powered safety mechanisms that prevent AI systems from showing violent and abusive content. The necessary work is called click work, which means labelling pictures or videos to train AI.
- Workers have to scan and label violent and graphic content so that AI companies can include ethical rules in their AI training. This work can be psychologically damaging. Workers from in the global south often do the click work under awful working conditions: low wages, no fixed contracts, exposure to violent content and a lack of psychosocial support.
- It's important to note that there is much human work behind AI applications' “clean” look, with often horrendous working conditions. Huge tech companies fail to take responsibility and outsource this problematic work to low-paid workers in the global south.
