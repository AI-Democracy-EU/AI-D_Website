Let’s say you have 9 million cat images and want to know how many different cat types are in the dataset. DL can analyse and structure the data so that in the output you can have a better idea of which different types of cats are in the dataset. However, AI does not know if the images show cats or anything else. Humans need to know the purpose (e.g., cat detection) and ensure that no other images (e.g. of other animals, of food, of cars) are involved. Without a clear rule, AI cannot distinguish that a frog is not a cat.
Conclusion:
- DL is useful to analyse large (unlabelled/unstructured) raw data sets where it’s not necessarily predefined what can be found. Hence, it can find patterns in large unstructured data sets and do the structuring and labelling automatically
- The use of DL is therefore productive for e.g. image processing and recognition, recognising speech, translation etc. Still, it's the human who needs to know what the output is and how useful it is.
