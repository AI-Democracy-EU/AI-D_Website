Although the Potentials of AI are great, they come with certain risks.
Privacy Concerns and Security Risks: Privacy and Security issues can arise. AI Systems collect and use large amounts of data. For example, assistants like Alexa gather audio information. Another example would be facial recognition applications. Besides privacy concerns, security risks are associated with AI’s use and potential misuse. Cybercriminals, for example, can leverage AI to create more sophisticated cyberattacks, evade security defences, and exploit system vulnerabilities.
