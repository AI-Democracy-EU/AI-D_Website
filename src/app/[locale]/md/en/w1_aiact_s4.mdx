- **Loss of Human Connection:** A big challenge with AI Systems is the possible loss of human touch and social interaction. AI-tutoring systems, for example, can work perfectly fine for some tasks, but they lack skills that a real teacher has, like empathy or humour.
- **Dependence on AI:** Excessive dependence on AI systems could result in diminished creativity, critical thinking, and human intuition. It is crucial to maintain a balance between AI-driven decision-making and human involvement.
- **Job Displacement:** AI-powered automation has the potential to cause job losses across different sectors, particularly impacting low-skilled workers. However, some evidence suggests that AI and other emerging technologies could generate more jobs than they replace. As these technologies continue to evolve and improve, the workforce must adapt by learning new skills to remain competitive, with low-skilled workers being especially vulnerable to these changes.
