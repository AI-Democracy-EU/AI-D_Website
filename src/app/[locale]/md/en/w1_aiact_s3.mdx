- **Ethical Dilemmas:** If AI Systems are used for automated decision-making, questions on human autonomy and agency arise. AI Systems have started to impact humans’ ability to act and make decisions. For example, some companies have begun to implement automated recruitment software. Sometimes, these systems work with biased training data, which can lead to unfair conditions in the recruitment process.
- **Bias and Discrimination:** Biased training data can amplify injustices and discrimination due to biased training data or algorithmic design.
