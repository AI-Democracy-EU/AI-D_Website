- AI systems are tools trained to imitate certain human capabilities (like processing and analysing information, logical thinking, planning, etc.). Artificial intelligence is not intelligent in the same way humans are.
- AI is a new form of automation that can repeat patterns based on statistical probabilities. With Automated Decision Making (ADM), decisions are made by algorithms to automate processes such as loan approvals, hiring, or personalised recommendations.
- Engineers design and train AI Systems. In this process, humans decide which information/data is fed to the AI System.
