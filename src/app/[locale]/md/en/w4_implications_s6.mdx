What impact can the use of an AI tutor have? To navigate the intricacies of this concern, we offer a set of questions for reflection and discussion amongst teachers, school heads and other education professionals. We invite you to answer the questions in the next three slides, and write a compass for the good and fair use of AI Tutors in your institution. Focus your answers on what is relevant and appropriate to your institution, the learners and teachers you work with. You can also download the whole list of reflective questions in the download section on the starting page.

- In this slide we focus on the topic of data protection: What level of transparency is necessary for AI tutors to maintain user trust?
- How can students' privacy be safeguarded in an environment where AI tutors continuously collect data?
- What does it mean for students to have all their learning data collected? Can they be easily controlled by the teachers? Do they win or lose autonomy?
- What can you do to protect your data when using AI applications?
