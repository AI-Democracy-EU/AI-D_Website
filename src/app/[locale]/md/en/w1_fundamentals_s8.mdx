Human in the Loop
-	Think of AI as an advanced autopilot system in an aeroplane, as shown in the video. It can handle many tasks autonomously but still requires human pilots to take off, land, and manage unexpected situations.
-	Similarly, behind every AI system are humans who train the AI with data and interact with it through a mechanism known as "Human in the Loop." This process allows humans to provide feedback when an AI model's predictions fall below a certain confidence level.
-	Just as pilots intervene when necessary, humans help AI systems learn from feedback and improve over time. "Human in the Loop" reflects a partnership where AI systems and humans work together to improve the performance of specific applications. Importantly, humans maintain control over AI systems, determining what AI can and cannot do—much like pilots ultimately command an aircraft, even when autopilot is engaged.
