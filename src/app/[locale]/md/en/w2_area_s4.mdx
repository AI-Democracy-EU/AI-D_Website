LLM is becoming a new basic technology that generates a lot of attention in all various fields of work and thus becomes more and more relevant in our private as well as work lives. GenAI is changing and developing already existing tools and is being integrated into them. Text editing or mail programs for instance implement writing assistants that spell and grammar check our writing but also improve or adjust it according to a chosen style. In general, it changes how we interact with computers and phones because we can interact with them in our natural language, just like with a friend. Overall, GenAI models are changing the ways we organize collaboration and processes or create various forms of content. Understanding their basic functionality and obtaining skills to operate them will be relevant for many areas of work but also in our private lives.
